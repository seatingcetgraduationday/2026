How distribution works

Two stations run together in parallel: Left wing (Blocks 1–3) receives certificates at Certificate Reception 1, while Right wing (Blocks 4–6) receives certificates simultaneously at Certificate Reception 2.

Order of movement (Outer blocks first, followed by inner): BLUE outer blocks (Blocks 1 & 6) go first, followed by GREEN middle blocks (Blocks 2 & 5), and finally RED inner blocks (Blocks 3 & 4). Within every block, the first student row goes first, followed sequentially by the next rows (`BLUE FRONT → BLUE LAST → GREEN FRONT → GREEN LAST → RED FRONT → RED LAST`).

Circulation Path: Students step out into their block's outer passage, walk forward and up the curved side stage entry to the Certificate Reception desk, then walk down the center return steps, return along their block's inner passage, and file back into their own seats.

Note on Faculty Seats: Faculty rows (Row 1 at the front and Rows 24–25 at the back) remain seated throughout the ceremony; stage movement is disabled for faculty.
