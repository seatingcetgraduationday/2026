How distribution works

Two stations run together. Blocks 1–3 receive at Station 1, blocks 4–6 at Station 2.

Order of movement (Outer blocks first, followed by inner): Outer blocks (Blocks 1 & 6, blue) proceed first, followed by middle blocks (Blocks 2 & 5, green), and finally inner centre blocks (Blocks 3 & 4, red). Within each block, the first row goes first, followed sequentially by the next rows. (Note: Faculty members remain seated and do not have stage movement.)

Out by the outer passage, up the side steps, receive your certificate, come down the centre return steps and back by the inner passage.
