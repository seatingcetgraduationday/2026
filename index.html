How distribution works

Two stations run together in parallel: Blocks 1–3 receive at Certificate Station 1 (via the Left Wait Area), and Blocks 4–6 receive at Certificate Station 2 (via the Right Wait Area).

Order of movement: Outer blocks (Blocks 1 & 6) proceed first, followed by middle blocks (Blocks 2 & 5), and inner blocks (Blocks 3 & 4). Within each block/department, the first row goes first, followed by the next row as soon as half of the previous row exits the stage.

Untangled Circulation Path: Students step out into their block's outer passage, walk up to the Wait Area until their name is called, ascend the side stage steps to receive their certificate, come down the center return steps, and walk back down their block's inner passage to their seats.
